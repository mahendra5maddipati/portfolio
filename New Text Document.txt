🌱 My Final Note — With No Hate, Just Clarity
I’ve made mistakes.
I’ve wasted time.
I’ve watched things I shouldn’t have.
And I’ve taken every consequence, quietly.

They called it karma.
They called it sin.
They said god had turned away from me.
Some even dragged religion into it —
as if god keeps a scorecard for those who are already down.
And yes, somehow, they even blamed the country.

It’s almost funny, how much weight they needed to throw — just to ignore my pain.

But maybe they were all right.
It was my karma.

But don’t forget — karma isn’t selective.
What I’ve faced, I’ve owned.
What you project, you will face too — in your own time.

I’m not angry.
I’m not broken.
But I’m no longer silent.

I’m building something now —
For the ones who had no help.
For those who were shamed into silence.
For the version of me they tried to erase.

I don’t need forgiveness.
I don’t need validation.
I’ve met myself. I’ve forgiven myself.
And that’s more than most ever do.

So yes — it was my karma.
But don’t forget yours.
