Roles & Responsibilities – Junior Middleware Integration Engineer

1. Ensures Operational Continuity of Middleware Applications
 - Monitors performance and stability of middleware platforms used in production environments.
 - Manages application uptime and proactively mitigates risks that may lead to downtime.
 - Configures alerting mechanisms and log monitors for real-time system health insights.
 - Coordinates with infrastructure teams to plan and execute maintenance windows without business disruption.

2. Executes Seamless Integration Between Frontend and Backend Systems
 - Facilitates the flow of structured data between client interfaces (React.js) and backend services (Node.js, PostgreSQL).
 - Develops middleware logic that bridges REST APIs with databases to ensure data persistence and integrity.
 - Maintains version control and modular architecture for scalable middleware logic.
 - Addresses integration gaps or sync delays between data entry points and backend storage layers.

3. Designs and Deploys Workflow Automation Solutions
 - Implements automation scripts using Python and SQL to reduce manual intervention across data operations.
 - Automates barcode and QR code mapping processes to align with production timelines.
 - Triggers automated alerts and data push notifications across systems to streamline user workflows.
 - Continuously refines scripts and routines to reduce errors, time, and resource consumption.

4. Manages and Verifies QR/Barcode Generation and Assignment
 - Oversees generation of QR codes and barcodes assigned to individual batches, product cases, or item series.
 - Validates code-to-product mappings to ensure correct associations in line with production batches.
 - Prevents duplication, mislabeling, or untracked inventory through robust verification checkpoints.
 - Coordinates with production teams to align code generation timelines with manufacturing cycles.

5. Delivers First-Line Support and Troubleshooting of Middleware Systems
 - Identifies and resolves application faults, system bottlenecks, and unexpected behavior during live production.
 - Uses logs, analytics, and console diagnostics to isolate issues and recommend permanent fixes.
 - Engages development teams for code-level intervention when application anomalies persist.
 - Documents resolution steps and integrates learning into team-wide knowledge bases.

6. Maintains and Audits Production Databases
 - Performs routine health checks, indexing, and query optimization in PostgreSQL and MongoDB databases.
 - Validates data integrity, correcting inconsistencies found during barcode mapping or system reconciliation.
 - Builds custom queries and stored procedures to support advanced reporting and data manipulation.
 - Ensures database performance under high data volumes through regular maintenance and monitoring.

7. Contributes to Continuous Middleware Enhancement
 - Evaluates current middleware architecture for improvement opportunities in speed, usability, and security.
 - Suggests and tests new feature rollouts aligned with operational or business needs.
 - Collaborates in Agile sprints and sprint retrospectives, contributing ideas for backlog refinement.
 - Benchmarks performance post-updates to ensure enhancements lead to measurable outcomes.

8. Executes Middleware Testing and Validation Protocols
 - Participates in integration and regression testing for middleware releases and patches.
 - Simulates real-world use cases to validate new feature behavior under production-like conditions.
 - Prepares test plans and collaborates with QA teams to finalize release readiness.
 - Logs defects, tracks resolution progress, and supports retesting for closure.

9. Engages in Multi-Disciplinary Collaboration
 - Coordinates with development, production, QA, and IT support to align middleware functionality with end-user needs.
 - Participates in daily stand-ups, weekly syncs, and interdepartmental checkpoints.
 - Provides technical support during implementation of new hardware/software that interacts with the middleware layer.
 - Supports production teams with middleware tool training and system understanding.

10. Maintains Documentation, Logs, and Compliance Readiness
 - Updates middleware system documentation including architecture diagrams, SOPs, and technical references.
 - Tracks incidents, fixes, and system updates in well-organized logs for audit readiness.
 - Aligns middleware configurations and changes with internal IT policies and external regulatory standards.
 - Assists in periodic internal and external audits by providing necessary data and procedural clarity.
